  \ecvsection{Research Interests}

  \ecvsection{Research Interests}

\ecvitem{}{
My research focuses on autonomous robotic systems that integrate probabilistic estimation, spatial perception, computer vision and robot manipulation. Throughout my career, I have combined methodological research on robot autonomy with application-oriented projects in industrial automation, logistics, underwater robotics and, more recently, precision agriculture.
}

\ecvtitle{}{Robot Localization, Mapping and Spatial Registration}

\ecvitem{}{
A major research theme concerns robot localization and mapping. During my PhD and subsequent collaboration with the University of Freiburg, I contributed to Graph-Based SLAM through an incremental optimizer for pose-graph estimation~\cite{grisetti2008icra,lodirizzini2009ras}, together with distributed and parallel formulations suitable for multi-robot systems and large-scale environments~\cite{lodirizzini2009iros,lodirizzini2010iros}. I also investigated the theoretical structure of pose-graph optimization and maximum-likelihood mapping~\cite{lodirizzini2009ecmr,lodirizzini2009icar}.
}

\ecvitem{}{
A second line of work concerns place recognition and map registration. Notable contributions include FALKO, one of the first effective keypoint detectors for 2D laser scans~\cite{kallasi2016ral}, the GLAROT and GRD descriptors for landmark-based place recognition~\cite{kallasi2016iros,lodirizzini2017iros,lodirizzini2019ral}, and the Angular Radon Spectrum (ARS), a geometric representation for robust rotation estimation and point-cloud registration~\cite{lodirizzini2018pr,lodirizzini2022ral,fontana2023sensors}. These works established a coherent research line on geometric representations for localization and registration that continues in current studies on globally optimal multi-scan alignment.
}

\ecvtitle{}{Perception, Computer Vision and Robotic Manipulation}

\ecvitem{}{
My research on perception addresses the extraction of geometric and semantic information from LiDAR, stereo vision, RGB-D and 3D sensing systems. I have investigated active perception and next-best-view planning for object reconstruction and manipulation~\cite{aleotti2012icra,aleotti2014jirs}, object recognition from stereo and depth data~\cite{oleari2013iccp,lodirizzini2014ias}, and the integration of multiple sensor modalities for robotic applications.
}

\ecvitem{}{
These methodologies have been applied to challenging manipulation scenarios. Within the PRIN MARIS project, I contributed to perception systems for underwater intervention robots, including object detection, segmentation and pose estimation under adverse sensing conditions~\cite{lodirizzini2015ijars,kallasi2015oceans,lodirizzini2017caee,simetti2018joe}. More recently, I have developed perception and manipulation systems for industrial mobile manipulators and automated warehouse operations~\cite{aleotti2021ap,chiaravalli2020etfa}.
}

\ecvitem{}{
Another long-standing topic is autonomous navigation for industrial AGVs. My contributions include safe navigation in dynamic warehouse environments~\cite{lodirizzini2012icinco}, calibration methodologies for tricycle and four-wheel vehicles~\cite{kallasi2017ras,galasso2019rcim}, and feature-based localization systems that avoid artificial infrastructure~\cite{lodirizzini2018irosws}. Several of these solutions have been transferred to industrial partners and deployed in operational logistics facilities.
}

\ecvtitle{}{Precision Agriculture and Field Robotics}

\ecvitem{}{
In recent years my research has increasingly focused on precision agriculture through regional projects (ALADIN, POSITIVE, AGRARIAN), the AGRITECH National Research Centre, and collaborations with agricultural stakeholders. The common objective is the development of autonomous robotic solutions for sustainable and data-driven agricultural operations.
}

\ecvitem{}{
A first research direction concerns decision support and planning for precision irrigation. I have contributed to methodologies for variable-rate irrigation based on environmental sensing and optimization techniques~\cite{amoretti2020smartsys,penzotti2023compag,penzotti2023ecpa}, enabling more efficient use of water resources in open-field cultivation.
}

\ecvitem{}{
A second direction addresses robotic harvesting and agricultural manipulation. Recent work includes perception and manipulation systems for autonomous tomato harvesting using mobile manipulators, soft grippers and force sensing~\cite{monica2024etfa,saccuti2026tfr}. In parallel, I have investigated crop monitoring and disease detection using multispectral imaging, with particular attention to vineyard applications~\cite{saccuti2025data}. These activities combine robotics, computer vision, machine learning and advanced sensing technologies to support the digital transformation of agriculture.
}
